My bag — your club distances
These are your clubs, and you change them here. The first three numbers are read straight off the game’s bag screen, one club at a time. If your clubs don’t match what shipped — different equipment, a different player, or the numbers simply moved — type your own over them. Everything the app tells you is built on these, so they are the ones worth getting right first. They save automatically.
Min carryThe shortest this club will fly. The lowest number the power dial will give you. Max carryThe furthest it flies through the air, before it lands. This is the big number on the club’s page in the game. Max totalWhere it finishes after landing and rolling out on a fairway. Always the larger of the two. Green rollHow much the club runs on a green compared with that fairway roll. 1.0 means it runs just as far; 0.5 means it checks up and rolls half as far; the 60° measures 0.00 and simply does not release. Five clubs are MEAS — measured from paired fairway and green runs in the same session — and the other eight are INTERP, worked out linearly on carry between those five. Interpolated is derived, not observed. The shot log will offer you a better number once you have logged five shots with a club.
Shot types — how each shot behaves
Every shot in the game flies differently. These three numbers are how the app knows the difference between a full approach and a pitch. A MEAS row was read off your screen; a GUESS row is my estimate, and those are the ones worth correcting first.
ReachHow far this shot goes as a share of the club’s full carry. 1.0 is everything the club has; 0.5 is half of it. A pitch with a 7 Iron reaches nowhere near a full 7 Iron, and this is the number that says so. WindHow hard the wind pushes this shot around. 1.0 takes the wind full in the face; 0.15 barely notices it. This is why a low pitch holds its line in a gale and a high flop does not. Roll ydYards this shot always rolls out after landing on a fairway, whichever club plays it. 0 means don’t use a fixed number — work the roll out from the club’s own carry-to-total ratio instead.
Landing surface — how much each one grabs
What the ball does when it comes down on each surface, measured against landing on a fairway. 1.0 rolls exactly as far as a fairway would give you. 0.55 rolls a little over half as far. 0.15 means it lands and stops more or less where it pitched. Fairway is your own number by definition; everything else is GUESS until you have tested it.
Wind & elevation — what conditions cost you
How much a mile per hour of wind, or a foot of slope, is worth in yards. From Operation Sports' tested 2K25 guide — the only published figures anywhere. 2K has never released a formula. If your own shots say otherwise, the shot log will offer you corrected numbers.
TailwindYards you gain for every mph of wind at your back.SRC
Headwind, approachYards you lose per mph into the wind, on anything shorter than the threshold below.SRC
Headwind, full shotSame, but on full shots — they are in the air longer, so wind costs more.SRC
Full-shot thresholdThe carry where a shot stops counting as an approach and the rate above takes over.GUESS

Quartering factorHow much of the wind counts when it is at 45° instead of straight on. 0.7 = seven tenths of it.SRC
ElevationFeet of climb that cost you one yard of carry. 3 means every 3 ft uphill plays a yard longer — and downhill gives it back.SRC
My windows
When you pick a club and a shot in the game, the power dial shows a highest and a lowest yardage. That pair is what tells the app a Punch with a 7 Iron tops out at 139 and won’t go below 30 — which is different from what the club does on a full swing, and that is why it can’t live in My bag. Pick a club, read the two numbers off your screen, type them in. A pair you fill in stops being a guess immediately.

Shot log
Every logged shot, newest first. The two figures are carry error and roll error in yards — positive means it went further than the model said. Nothing lateral is recorded: distance is the part people judge reliably. A shot marked your pick is one where you overruled the recommendation; it is kept and exported in full, but it can’t be scored against a prediction made for a different club, so it is left out of the suggestions.
